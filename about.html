our story

From a busted apartment oven to your favorite order

Cookiez wasn't supposed to be a business. It was supposed to be a distraction. It's been a very good distraction.

It started in a college apartment with an oven that ran twenty degrees hot and a habit of baking instead of studying. After maybe the fortieth test batch — brown butter, more salt than felt reasonable, chilled dough, underbaked on purpose — something finally clicked. Friends started asking for a dozen "for a study break." Then friends of friends. Then people who weren't friends of anyone, which is when it stopped being a hobby.

The name was the easy part. Everything else — figuring out a commercial kitchen, learning to bake three hundred cookies without burning the first batch, saying yes to a real storefront — took a lot longer, and involved a lot more trial and error than anyone tells you about.

How we got here

  1. 2021

    Dorm kitchen, forty test batches

    One bad oven and way too much brown butter. This is where the recipes actually started.

  2. 2022

    First farmers market table

    Sold out in ninety minutes. Came back the next week with triple the dough and sold out again.

  3. 2023

    Moved into a real commercial kitchen

    Traded the apartment oven for six full-size sheet pans at once. Hired the first two bakers.

  4. 2024

    Opened the storefront

    A tiny space with a big case, built around one idea: cookies should still be warm when you get them.

  5. Today

    Same recipes, still stubborn about them

    Real butter, small batches, nothing frozen and shipped in. That part was never up for negotiation.

What we're about

Real ingredients, always

Real butter, real vanilla, real chocolate. If it needs a lab to explain, it's not going in the dough.

Baked same-day

Nothing sits in a freezer waiting for an order. Dough is made and baked the same day it's sold.

Weird flavor ideas welcome

Most of our best sellers started as someone saying "what if we just tried it" in the kitchen group chat.
